## Redundant words

Delete the word in brackets.

[forward] planning; [proactively] monitors; [advance] reservations; small [in size]; blend [together]; collaborate [together]; [critically] important; [critical] assessments; [current] incumbent; during [the period] 2021–2023; time [period]; during [the course of]; [end] result; is [in the process of] reforming; [future] prospects; improve [over time]; the [exact] same; has [actually] declined; agreed [upon]; [new] innovation; [past] experience; a [real] danger; recur [again]; revert [back]; [temporary] reprieve; [a total of] 15 schools; [All things being equal,]; [Basically,]; rehabilitate the [existing] roads; three towns[, namely]: ; in [the months of] December and January; [completely] destroyed; consensus [of opinion]; [knowledgeable] experts; close [proximity] to; the [general] public; dates [back] to; completed [to date]; slowed [down]; seven [different] ministries; each [and every]; [foreign] imports; will be [located] in; [in order] to; the [main] focus; [specific] details; each [individual] subproject; whether [or not].

Also usually removable: "relevant" (relevant government officials), "appropriate" (appropriate education materials), "level", "phase", "stage", "sector", "subsector", and statements of the obvious ("increased by more than 1 million, from 50.3 million to 51.6 million").

## Overused words and alternatives

| --- | --- |

| improved | better, cleaner, more effective, more efficient, more sustainable, quicker, stronger |

Not italicized, because they are in Merriam-Webster: ad hoc, per capita, milieu, vice versa, e.g., i.e., et al.

## Spelling and hyphenation

Closed: antimonopoly, cofinance, crosscutting, groundwater, interagency, landlocked, macroeconomic, microfinance, midterm, moneylender, multisector, multitranche, nonbank, nongovernment, nonperforming, nonregional, nonrevenue, nonsovereign, nontariff, offshore, online, onlend, overpopulated, pipeline, postwar, prequalification, reallocate, relend, semiannual, shareholding, socioeconomic, stakeholder, subproject, subprogram, subregion, underbudgeted, waterborne.

Hyphenated: e-banking, e-learning, e-procurement (but email); self-funded; non-European, sub-Saharan, pre-1950; multi-industry, non-oil, pro-poor, re-lent, semi-independent; anti-money-laundering legislation; cost-effective, risk-averse, short-lived, time-sensitive; vice-president; back-to-office report; value-at-risk; middle-income country; state-owned enterprise; small and medium-sized enterprises; policy-based loan; results-based lending; climate-proofing.

Phrasal adjectives with a hyphen before the noun: across-the-board increases, asset-backed securities, capacity-building activities, community-based organization, income-generating activities, mass-produced items, non-English-speaking applicants, second-largest city, 52-year-old man.

Phrasal adjectives without: capacity development plans, clean energy project, energy sector reforms, environmental protection measures, foreign exchange rate, power generation capacity, power supply company, private equity fund, private sector financing, upper management decision.

En dash compounds: ADB–OECD study, Bangkok–Manila flight, cost–benefit analysis, debt–equity ratio, demand–supply gap, public–private partnership, Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease, US dollar–won exchange rate.

Computer terms: artificial intelligence (AI); computer (not PC); email; Excel spreadsheet, PowerPoint presentation, Word document; information and communication technology (ICT); IT; internet, intranet; internet service provider (ISP); PDF; PIN (not PIN number); SMS; voice over internet protocol (VOIP); web (not World Wide Web); ATM (not ATM machine).
