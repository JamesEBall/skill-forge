# ADB member names and currency units

From Appendix 1 of the ADB *Handbook of Style and Usage* (2024 edition, online version updated 13 October 2026). Names and currencies change; the online handbook is the authority.

## Rules

- Use the short name unless a template explicitly calls for the long name.
- Members marked "the" take the definite article in running text and drop it in tables and figures.
- Member codes appear only inside loan and TA numbers (TA 9499-LAO), never as a name.
- In alphabetical lists the People's Republic of China goes under C, the Republic of Korea under K, and the Federated States of Micronesia under M.
- Table and figure forms for inverted names: "China, People's Republic of"; "Korea, Republic of"; "Micronesia, Federated States of".
- Formal government name is "Government of [short name]" with "the" where the short name takes it, except where shown below.
- Currency symbol directly before the amount, no space. Currency names are lowercase.
- Non-members bordering members: Democratic People's Republic of Korea; Russian Federation or Russia.

## Currency notes

- Singular and plural symbol forms for rupees: NRe1 but NRs10; PRe1 but PRs10; SLRe1 but SLRs420. In front matter: "Pakistan rupee/s (PRe/PRs)".
- Low-value units in a currency equivalents table: no more than two decimal places to the right of the last zero. D1.00 = $0.000063; $1.00 = D15,944.50.
- Front-matter note: "In this report, "$" refers to United States dollars." Add ", unless otherwise stated" when other dollar currencies appear.
- SDR is used as a symbol (SDR2.15 million) and is listed in the abbreviations list. In prose: special drawing rights (SDRs).
