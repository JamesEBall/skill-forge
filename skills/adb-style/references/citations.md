# Footnote references

From the References, Footnotes, Cross-References, Quotations, and Copyright entries of the ADB *Handbook of Style and Usage* (2024 edition).

## General rules

- Board documents cite sources in footnotes; no bibliography. Publications may use a bibliography and may follow Chicago.
- Elements are separated by periods. Titles of whole works are italic and in title case; titles of articles and chapters are in title case, roman. Keep the original spelling of titles.
- Authors: initial and surname (J. Sachs). Two or three authors are all named; more than three become "A. Berger et al."
- Page numbers are optional, at the end: p. 18; pp. 275–277 (en dash).
- Extra information goes in parentheses at the end. Be consistent: if one project reference gives the loan amount, all do.
- Abbreviate an organization's name if the abbreviation has already been defined in the text or an earlier footnote; otherwise spell out and define.
- Several references in one footnote: separate with semicolons, with "and" after the last semicolon.
- A URL may be written out at the end or hyperlinked on the title. A bare URL is not a reference. Do not break URLs. Punctuate normally after a URL.
- Access dates only for online databases: "(accessed 29 June 2023)".
- Hold online sources to the same standard as print. Wikipedia is not an acceptable source.
- No footnote needed in Board documents for frequently cited ADB policies ("The Environmental and Social Framework (2024, as amended to date)") or for national laws given with their year ("The Bangladesh Water Act, 2013").
- Repeat citation: "(footnote 8)" in the text. With a page number: a new footnote reading "Footnote 8, pp. 19–25." If the earlier footnote held several sources, cite the one source again in a new footnote. Never ibid. or op. cit.
- Quotations need a footnote with the page number. Paraphrases need a footnote and no quotation marks.
- Short quotations, tables, and figures from other sources may be used with a citation. Anything that is a substantial part of the source needs the copyright owner's permission.

## Models

Italics are shown with asterisks.

### ADB projects

```
ADB. Country: Project Name. URL
ADB. Lao People's Democratic Republic: Sustainable Natural Resource Management and Productivity Enhancement Project. http://www.adb.org/projects/37579-032/main ($20 million).
ADB. 2023. *Technical Assistance for Private Sector Data for Development* (TA 10112-REG).
```

Cite the project by its full title at first mention in the text and footnote the project website. Do not cite by loan or TA number in the text. If the document has a table listing all projects, one general footnote can replace the individual ones.

## Sources under tables and figures

- "Source:" or "Sources:" on the last line below the table or figure. Several sources in alphabetical order by author, separated by semicolons with "and" before the last.
- Give the document, not just the organization: "Source: International Monetary Fund (IMF). 2023. *Indonesia: 2023 Article IV Consultation—Press Release; Staff Report; and Statement by the Executive Director for Indonesia.* IMF Staff Country Report. No. 23/221."
- An organization's name alone is enough for unpublished data it supplied to ADB.
- ADB's own estimates: "Source: Asian Development Bank estimates." or a department name. Never "Staff estimates".
