# Abbreviations and units

From the Abbreviations and Units of Measurement entries and Appendix 3 of the ADB *Handbook of Style and Usage* (2024 edition).

## Abbreviations list (front matter)

- Heading "Abbreviations". Alphabetical by abbreviation; entries starting with a number or special character first.
- Format: `ADB – Asian Development Bank`. Spelled-out form lowercase unless a proper noun: `GDP – gross domestic product`.
- Singular forms, except those that only make sense in the plural (SMEs, MSMEs, IRRs).
- Wording, spelling, and punctuation must match the text.
- Leave out abbreviations that appear only in a table, figure, box, or annex and are defined there, and well-known ones used fewer than three times.
- Non-English: `Bappenas – Kementerian Perencanaan Pembangunan Nasional/Badan Perencanaan Pembangunan Nasional (Ministry of National Development Planning/National Development Planning Agency)`; `MSF – Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders)`.
- Units of measurement go under a separate heading "Weights and Measures" if there are more than three; otherwise in the abbreviations list.

## Units of measurement

ADB uses the metric system. Other systems are acceptable where they are the industry standard (British thermal units for gas). Chinese units (mu) may be used with the metric equivalent in a footnote or glossary.

Rules: spell out at first use with the abbreviation in parentheses if used often; singular only (22 kg); no period (5,960 ha); a space between number and unit; no hyphen with an abbreviated unit (11 kV line, 50 ha field) but a hyphen when spelled out as an adjective (11-kilovolt line, 50-hectare field). Exception: bp (basis point), bps (basis points).
